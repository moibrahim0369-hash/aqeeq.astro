## Aqeeq Astro interpretation layer

Future original writing should remain a separate layer from school labels and calculations. The content model is: verified source term (with citation/status), Arabic helper label, stable ID, formula and review status, calculated result, then original Aqeeq content (meaning, strengths, challenges, concrete examples, reflection prompts, and development steps). No paid source copy is to be reproduced. Psychosomatic associations must remain symbolic and must not be represented as diagnosis or medical prediction.
